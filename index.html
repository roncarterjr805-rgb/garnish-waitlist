Why Garnish

Everything you wish your recipe app did

We built Garnish around the features people actually want — not just a list of recipes.

🎰
Recipe Roulette

Can't decide what to cook? Spin the slot machine and let fate pick your dinner. Filter by category, time, and calories.

🛒
Amazon Fresh Delivery

Every recipe has a one-tap "Get all ingredients delivered" button. Opens Amazon Fresh with every item ready to add to your cart.

👨‍🍳
Chef View

One step at a time in large text. Screen stays on automatically so it never dims while your hands are full of flour.

⚖️
Ingredient Scaler

Cooking for 1 or feeding 12? Tap + or − and every ingredient quantity rescales automatically, including fractions.

🔍
Ingredient Filter

Search by what you have. Exclude what you hate. Type "no mushrooms" and every recipe with mushrooms disappears.

💡
Community Tips

Upvote-sorted cooking tweaks from real users. "Halve the sugar" or "add a pinch of smoked paprika" — always shown most helpful first.

How it works

From craving to clean plate

Three steps is all it takes.

1
Pick a recipe

Browse 632 healthy recipes by category, or spin the Recipe Roulette to let fate decide. Filter out ingredients you don't like.

2
Order ingredients

One tap opens Amazon Fresh with every ingredient ready. Schedule Whole Foods delivery for the same day or next morning.

3
Cook with confidence

Follow the step-by-step cooking mode. Built-in timers, large text, screen stays on. Your phone works as hard as you do.
